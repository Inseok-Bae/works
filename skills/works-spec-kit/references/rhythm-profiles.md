# Rhythm Profiles

Use rhythm as part of the concept, not as a polish layer.

## Profiles

- `slow-drift`
  - Best for: residue, memory, fog, hesitation
  - Signal: low-frequency change, long trails, sparse logs
- `queued-latency`
  - Best for: regret, deferred consequence, social lag
  - Signal: intention queues, staggered reactions, indirect causality
- `cyclic-return`
  - Best for: recurrence, waking/sleeping, seasonal pressure
  - Signal: pulses, phases, partial repetition with drift
- `threshold-collapse`
  - Best for: overheat, fracture, saturation, rupture
  - Signal: nonlinear cost, sudden lock, hard transitions
- `one-shot-loop`
  - Best for: plots that must end, dry, empty, or conclude
  - Signal: explicit start, phase envelope, end latch, restart path
- `ambient-autonomy`
  - Best for: worlds where the visitor should feel secondary
  - Signal: self-running field, sparse intervention windows

## Selection Rule

- Write rhythm in the spec before choosing animations.
- If two consecutive pieces share the same rhythm profile, force divergence on substrate or visitor role.
